# SBC Analysis

Source: https://dashboard.dev2.mybrainguide.org/questionnaire/sbc/

SBC — Speech Based Cognitive assessment. A self-administered speech task that produces a continuous risk score from 0 to 1 (higher = lower risk). Result routes to one of three tiers: 0.5 and up = Low Risk, 0.2-0.5 = Medium Risk, under 0.2 = High Risk.
