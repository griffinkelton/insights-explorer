## Assessment Scores by Flow Type

AD8 — Lower score = less concern (0-1 Good, 2-8 Poor)
MIS — Higher score = better memory. Score = (2 x Free Recall) + Cued Recall (5-8 Good, 0-4 Poor)
SBC — Speech Based Cognition (0-1, higher = lower risk). Thresholds: >0.5 Low Risk, 0.2-0.5 Medium Risk, <0.2 High Risk. Most SBC respondents do not receive a scored result.
