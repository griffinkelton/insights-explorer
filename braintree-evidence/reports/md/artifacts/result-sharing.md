Source: https://dashboard.dev2.mybrainguide.org/questionnaire/sharing/

Every click of the "Share PDF via Email" button on a persona/result page. No email address is ever shown here — only whether a share happened, which result page it was for, when, and whether the person opted in to be contacted again.

"Opted In to Be Contacted" reflects the "keep in touch" checkbox shown at share time — an explicit, person-level consent signal. This is the only contactability data available in this report; the email address itself is never modeled or displayed here. To actually reach someone, an authorized query against the raw source (`usa2-brainguide.MyBrainguide.raw_share-pdf-via-email`, filtered to the same date range / result page) is required, run outside this dashboard.
