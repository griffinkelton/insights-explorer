Note: "Exit rate" here differs from GA4's "bounce rate." Exit means the session had no further page view after this page, built purely from page-view sequence. A visitor can be highly engaged (GA4 counts a session as engaged after 10 seconds, a second page, or a conversion) yet still register an "exit" here, because questionnaire steps, PDF saves, audio plays, and outbound clicks fire events rather than new page loads.
