The Campaign Story — Part 1

Cross-Network Didn't Grow — It Got Reclassified

GA4's "Cross-network" bucket captures Performance Max and Demand Gen campaigns that serve one ad across Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, and Discover.

PMax adoption jumped from 60% to 71% of advertisers between 2024–2025 industry-wide.

What this explains

613 → 183,055 sessions (+29,762%)

Consistent with turning on/scaling an automated campaign — not organic growth. Also explains why Paid Search stayed flat.

Industry context: Hallam Agency, MRS Digital, SEOTesting analyses of GA4 cross-network behavior (2024–2026).

The Campaign Story — Part 2

The Surge Lines Up With a Real Campaign Push

UsAgainstAlzheimer's ran a named BrainGuide campaign in Hispanic Heritage Month 2024

Targeted El Paso & Phoenix, explicitly spotlighting MyBrainGuide.org with digital ads and community partners.

CDC-funded National Healthy Brain Initiative has been active since 2020

5-year cooperative agreement funds tailored digital dissemination to Black and Latino communities.

This reframes paid concentration as intentional outreach — with an open question

Is Cross-network/Display targeting actually reaching women, Black, and Hispanic audiences, per Client Question #2 & #11 below?

Source: usagainstalzheimers.org press release (Oct 2024); Center for Brain Health Equity CDC cooperative agreement documentation.

The Depth Story

Small Channels, Deepest Trust

Referral — 1.1% of sessions

~9:51 avg. session

Benchmarking shows organic search typically carries the strongest engagement of any channel — underinvested relative to quality.

10 Reasoned Takeaways — Part 1

1. Traffic quadrupled — a funded campaign push

Sessions rose 302%, consistent with named campaigns and CDC-funded outreach, not organic drift.

2. Pages/session nearly doubled — content-rich campaign design

Culturally tailored messaging favors multi-format, layered content.

3. Cross-network exploded — GA4's PMax bucket, not new audience

613 → 183,055 sessions reflects a shift into Performance Max/Demand Gen campaign types.

4. Display became a core engine — same automated-campaign effect

+1,290% growth mirrors broader PMax/Demand Gen adoption (60%→71%, 2024-2025).

5. Paid Search flattened — budget reallocated, not lost

-6.2% YoY is the expected mirror image of the cross-network shift.

10 Reasoned Takeaways — Part 2

6. Paid concentration (91.5%) is a targeting-transparency risk

Automated campaigns don't expose demographics — directly relevant to Client Question #2 and #11.

7. Organic Search is under-scaled but proven to convert attention into time

+193% growth and ~5 min sessions align with benchmark data on organic performance.

8. Referral is the most trusted channel — a known pattern in this field

~10 min average duration matches published trusted-messenger findings.

9. Homepage dominance reflects broad-match campaign design

Automated campaigns default to homepage landings, diluting topic-level demand signals.

10. The 94.2% engagement rate needs a tracking audit before it's trusted

Far outside nonprofit (43%) and higher-ed (63%) benchmarks — a config change is the likely cause.
