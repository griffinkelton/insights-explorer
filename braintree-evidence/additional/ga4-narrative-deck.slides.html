The Campaign Story — Part 1

Cross-Network Didn't Grow — It Got Reclassified and Refunded

GA4's "Cross-network" bucket exists specifically to capture Performance Max and Demand Gen campaigns — automated Google Ads products that serve one ad across Search, Display, YouTube, Gmail, and Discover simultaneously.

PMax adoption jumped from 60% to 71% of advertisers between 2024 and 2025 industry-wide. When a media buyer shifts budget into PMax, GA4 reports nearly all of that traffic as "Cross-network," not "Search" or "Display."

What this explains

613 → 183,055 sessions (+29,762%)

A change this size is consistent with turning on or scaling a new automated campaign type — not gradual audience growth. It also explains why Paid Search stayed flat: budget likely shifted between Google Ads campaign types rather than growing overall spend.

Industry context: Hallam Agency, MRS Digital, and SEOTesting analyses of GA4 cross-network channel behavior (2024–2026).

The Campaign Story — Part 2

The Traffic Surge Lines Up With a Real Campaign Push

UsAgainstAlzheimer's ran a named BrainGuide campaign in 2026

Targeted at Hispanic communities in El Paso and Phoenix, timed to Hispanic Heritage Month, explicitly spotlighting MyBrainGuide.org with digital advertising and community partners.

CDC-funded National Healthy Brain Initiative has been active since 2020

A 5-year cooperative agreement funds tailored digital dissemination to Black and Latino communities — a plausible source of sustained paid media investment behind this year's numbers.

This context reframes the paid-channel concentration as intentional, not accidental

If this growth is campaign-funded outreach to priority populations, the next question for the Evaluation is whether Cross-network/Display targeting is actually reaching women, Black, and Hispanic audiences — not just driving volume.

Source: usagainstalzheimers.org press release (Oct 2024) and Center for Brain Health Equity CDC cooperative agreement documentation.

The Depth Story

Small Channels, Deepest Trust

Referral — 1.1% of sessions

~9:51 avg. session

Academic and CDC-funded research on reaching Black and Latino communities on Alzheimer's finds that messages delivered via trusted community partners — faith leaders, promotoras, minority-serving providers — consistently outperform generic ads in depth of engagement, because visitors arrive already primed and motivated.

Organic Search — 2.3% of sessions

+193% YoY, ~5 min avg.

Nonprofit and higher-ed benchmarking shows organic search traffic typically carries the strongest engagement of any channel (often 60%+ engagement rate) — a signal this channel is underinvested relative to its quality.

Read on this: the highest-intent visitors are arriving through the channels getting the least budget. Referral partnerships and SEO deserve real investment, not just paid media scale.

Sources: Gerontologist (2025) on engaging African American and Latino communities; EAB and Wired Impact nonprofit/higher-ed GA4 benchmarking studies.

10 Reasoned Takeaways — Part 1

1. Traffic quadrupled — a funded campaign push

Sessions rose 302%, consistent with UsAgainstAlzheimer's named BrainGuide campaign and CDC-funded outreach, not organic drift.

2. Pages/session nearly doubled — content-rich campaign design

Culturally tailored messaging guidance favors multi-format, layered content, which naturally produces multi-page visits.

3. Cross-network exploded — GA4's PMax bucket, not new audience

613 → 183,055 sessions reflects a shift into Performance Max/Demand Gen campaign types, which GA4 groups under Cross-network.

4. Display became a core engine — same automated-campaign effect

+1,290% growth mirrors the broader PMax/Demand Gen adoption trend (60%→71% of advertisers, 2024-2025).

5. Paid Search flattened — budget likely reallocated, not lost

-6.2% YoY is the expected mirror image when spend shifts into cross-network campaign types.

10 Reasoned Takeaways — Part 2

6. Paid concentration (91.5%) is a targeting-transparency risk

Automated campaigns don't expose demographic detail, which matters directly for the "are we reaching priority populations" research question.

7. Organic Search is under-scaled — but proven to convert attention into time

+193% growth and ~5 min sessions align with benchmark data showing organic consistently outperforms paid on engagement.

8. Referral is the most trusted channel — a known pattern in this field

~10 min average duration matches published findings on trusted-messenger outreach to Black and Latino communities.

9. Homepage dominance reflects broad-match campaign design

Automated campaigns often default to homepage landings rather than topic-matched pages, diluting apparent topic-level demand.

10. The 94.2% engagement rate needs a tracking audit before it's trusted

It sits far outside nonprofit (43%) and higher-ed (63%) benchmarks — a config change is the most likely explanation.
