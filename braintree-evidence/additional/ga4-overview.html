10 Takeaways — Part 1

1. Traffic quadrupled

Sessions rose 302%, adding ~278,000 visits YoY.

2. Content consumption grew faster than traffic

Page views up 702%; pages/session nearly doubled to 2.75.

3. Cross-network is the single biggest change agent

613 → 183,055 sessions; now 49.5% of all traffic.

4. Display became a core acquisition engine

+1,290% growth; now a quarter of all sessions.

5. Paid Search is comparatively flat

-6.2% YoY — no longer the dominant paid channel.

10 Takeaways — Part 2

6. Paid mix concentration is high-risk

91.5% of sessions from 3 paid channels — heavy dependency.

7. Organic Search is under-scaled but promising

+193% growth, ~5 min average session — SEO opportunity.

8. Referral traffic is the most engaged

Only 1.1% of sessions but ~10 min average duration.

9. Homepage remains the front door

240,243 landing sessions (65%); trials & provider finder lead secondary entries.

10. The engagement jump needs validation

43.0% → 94.2% is unusually large — audit tracking/config before trusting it fully.

Recommended Next Steps

🔧 Fix attribution

Correct malformed UTMs and the "goole" misspelling.

🔍 Audit the engagement spike

Confirm no GA4 event/consent config change inflated the rate.

🧭 Map true exit points

Run GA4 Path Exploration from top 3 landing pages.

🌱 Diversify beyond paid

Invest in SEO and referral partnerships given strong engagement.
